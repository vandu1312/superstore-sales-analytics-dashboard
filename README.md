# superstore-sales-analytics-dashboard
Objective

Performed end-to-end sales and profitability analysis to identify revenue drivers, margin leakage, and discount impact using Power BI.

🛠 Tools Used

Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Dashboarding)

Excel (Initial data validation)

DAX (Profit Margin calculation)

Key Insights

West region generated highest revenue driven primarily by Consumer segment.

Overall profit margin stands at ~12%.

High discount levels correlate with profit volatility and negative margins.

Revenue distribution across customers is moderately concentrated.

Identified high-revenue customers contributing low profit due to aggressive discounting.
