# Sales and Profitability Analysis Dashboard: Superstore Dataset

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales and profitability using the Superstore dataset. The objective was to identify key revenue drivers, evaluate profit margins, assess the impact of discounting strategies, and uncover factors contributing to margin leakage.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to provide business stakeholders with actionable insights into sales performance, customer profitability, and regional trends.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze sales and profit performance across regions, segments, and customers
* Identify key revenue drivers and profit contributors
* Evaluate the impact of discounting on profitability
* Detect margin leakage and profit volatility patterns
* Design an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains transactional retail data with the following attributes:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer ID and Segment
* Region and Location
* Product Category and Sub-Category
* Sales Revenue
* Profit
* Discount

This dataset represents retail operations across multiple regions and customer segments.

## Tools and Technologies Used

* Power BI – dashboard development and data visualization
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – calculated measures and KPIs

---

## Key Performance Metrics Calculated

The following business KPIs were developed:

**Profit Margin**
Profit Margin = Profit / Sales

**Total Revenue**
Sum of total sales across all transactions

**Customer Profitability**
Profit contribution by individual customers

**Discount Impact Analysis**
Relationship between discount levels and profitability

---

## Key Insights and Findings

* The West region generated the highest revenue, driven primarily by the Consumer segment
* Overall profit margin was approximately 12%, indicating moderate operational efficiency
* High discount levels were strongly correlated with profit volatility and negative margins
* Revenue distribution was moderately concentrated among a subset of customers
* Several high-revenue customers contributed low profit due to aggressive discounting strategies

The Power BI dashboard includes the following pages:

### Discount and Profitability Analysis

* Profit margin by discount level

### Regional and Customer Insights

* Revenue and profit by region
* Customer-level profitability analysis
* Segment-wise performance comparison

---

## Business Impact and Recommendations

The analysis revealed that excessive discounting significantly reduces profitability despite increasing revenue.

Optimizing discount strategies and focusing on high-margin customer segments could improve overall profitability and reduce margin leakage.
